## The 6 ms mode: found and fixed

The ~6 ms mode above was not a harness artifact. The ring signalled the
writer as the first statement of its publish, while still holding the lock,
and the woken writer made a single attempt to take that lock. It collided
with the producer that had just woken it about half the time, and a writer
that misses waits for its 5 ms safety retry — which is exactly the mode,
with the 5 ms timer plus scheduling showing up as 6 ms.

Two changes remove it. The wakeup is claimed under the lock but sent after
it is released, so a writer is never woken into a lock it cannot take; and
the consumer now gets the same bounded yield budget a producer has, so a
genuine overlap is absorbed in microseconds instead of waiting for a timer.
The retry timer stays as the last-resort safety net it was meant to be.
